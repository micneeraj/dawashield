# Why DawaShield

**DawaShield answers one question for Indian families: "Is the medicine in my home on an official CDSCO quality alert?"**: by voice in Hindi or English offline (Punjabi, Gujarati, Marathi, Kannada, Tamil online), on cheap phones, with the official source on every answer.

## What makes it different

1. **Voice-first in Indian languages.** Hindi and English offline; Punjabi, Gujarati, Marathi, Kannada and Tamil online. Say the medicine name; garbled speech-to-text ("पैरासाइट परासीईटीअमोल") is matched offline to the right name and confirmed with big buttons.
2. **Works offline on cheap Android phones.** The full official list (4,488 records, ~0.5 MB compressed) lives on the phone; matching is plain code, no AI model needed.
3. **Official CDSCO data, source on every answer.** Alert month, official link and record ID on every result; hand-verified records carry a "Verified" badge.
4. **Home medicine cabinet with automatic later alerts.** CDSCO lists come out about a month late, so saved medicines are re-checked every day and a push notification arrives if one is flagged later, even with the app closed.
5. **Family alerts.** WhatsApp message, or a push alert on a son's or daughter's phone after they accept an invite.
6. **Complaint-message helper.** A ready-to-send message for CDSCO with the official email and toll-free number.
7. **No ads, never recommends medicines.** DawaShield sells nothing and never suggests another medicine, dose or treatment.
8. **Careful about false alarms.** RED only when batch *and* company match with confidence (0.0% false-RED on 489 non-RED cases). Same company, different medicine (e.g. Dolo by Micro Labs, when only Micro Labs Amlodipine is listed) is GREY with "This alert is for a different medicine, not your batch."
9. **Honest results.** Grey never means "safe": "No official alert for this exact batch. This does not prove the medicine is safe. Official alerts can be delayed." Counterfeits can copy batch numbers. An in-app Transparency page shows the measured numbers and what is not yet proven.

## Who it is for

Elderly people and their families, including people who are not comfortable with English or typing, on low-cost Android phones with patchy internet.

## What it is not

Not a test of the medicine, not medical advice, not a pharmacy. It does not replace a doctor, pharmacist or official advice.
